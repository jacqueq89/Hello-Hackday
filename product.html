The problem

The events exist. Finding your people there is the hard part.

Armenian events in the UK are scattered across Facebook, Instagram, Armenopole and organiser websites. Newcomers don't know where to look, people off social media miss them entirely, and nobody can tell from a listing whether they'll actually meet anyone.

How it works

From "what's on?" to "see you there" in three steps

Find what's worth going to

Browse by date, map or distance in miles. Every event gets a rating out of 5 that puts meeting people first, then Armenian music and food.

See who's going

Spot Armenians in your industry, plus who's open to helping, looking for advice, or open to work.

Say hello, or go together

Send a one-off hello before the event, or join a small Don't go alone group that meets on WhatsApp first.

What's in the prototype

Built for meeting people, not just listing events

Every event, one feed

Search, filter by city, and sort by soonest, top rated or nearest. Real prices, including booking fees.

Map and distances

Pins for every venue, your location or chosen area, distances in miles, and one-tap directions.

A rating that means something

Networking 35%, culture 25%, people 15%, practical 15%, reputation 10%. Attendees' answers after events feed it.

Who's going, by industry

Profiles show only name, industry, job title and status.

Who it's for

Armenians across the UK, wherever they're starting from

Newcomers

Just moved, no network yet, don't know the groups.

Needs: a way in, and a first friendly face.

UK-born

Family ties, but feeling a little distant from the community.

Needs: a reason to go, and people their age.

Off social media

Miss everything posted only on Facebook or Instagram.

Needs: events by WhatsApp, email or a link.

Early career

Want to meet Armenians in their field.

Needs: someone open to helping, at the next event.

Why it's hard to copy

A search engine can list events. It can't tell you who'll be there.

Who's going, who's open to helping, who you met last time, and how an event really felt only exist because members share them. That's data no web search or AI agent can produce.

Privacy, built in

  • Only members who choose to appear are shown.
  • No company names or social links on profiles.
  • A hello must be accepted before anyone can chat.
  • Your device location is used for distances and never stored.

Organisers

Good for the people putting events on, too
